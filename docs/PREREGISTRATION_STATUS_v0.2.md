# Preregistration status v0.2

## Current status

The OSF Project is a private editable research record. It is not a formal preregistration. Until the project becomes public, it should display this statement:

> OSF Project private; public release pending. Formal preregistration pending post-pilot protocol freeze. No main-study results are reported.

## Registration gate

Submit a formal registration only after:

- the excluded annotation pilot and adjudication are complete;
- the codebook, model-output schema, and extraction prompt are promoted to v1.0;
- final sampling, collection-QA, deduplication, source-balance, and eligibility rules are frozen;
- exact extraction models, versions, settings, and access dates are fixed;
- the English translation system and preservation assessment are fixed;
- gold annotation, agreement, normalization, matching, and adjudication rules are fixed;
- estimands, denominators, exclusions, bootstrap procedure, and reporting rules are fixed;
- retry, failure, syntax-repair, and canonical-run rules are fixed; and
- ethics, data management, institutional-review status, retention, and release tiers are resolved.

The registration should precede final-test gold annotation and main extraction runs. If any relevant work has already begun or been inspected when registration occurs, the registration must state that transparently rather than implying a fully prospective design.

## Version and deviation policy

The registered record will never be silently replaced. Each later deviation must record its date, rationale, timing relative to outcome inspection, affected materials, and whether the resulting analysis is confirmatory, robustness, or exploratory.

## Recommended future registration contents

1. final preregistration protocol;
2. final scope and research questions;
3. frozen sampling and annotation procedures;
4. codebook, schemas, and prompt v1.0;
5. model and translation-system specification;
6. statistical analysis plan;
7. ethics and data-governance plan;
8. pilot-to-registration changelog; and
9. checksum manifest.
