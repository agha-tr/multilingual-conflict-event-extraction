- synchronized public packages for GitHub and the editable OSF Project;

### Clarified

- the benchmark has no news-only rule;
- public social media may be eligible after collection QA;
- the older mixed OSINT/HUMINT archive is not the benchmark sampling frame;
- faithfulness to one source is not real-world verification;
- Urdu and Pashto inclusion is a substantive design feature, not a predetermined deficit claim;
- live source registries, acquisition queries, candidate data, annotations, translations, model outputs, and results remain private at this stage.
